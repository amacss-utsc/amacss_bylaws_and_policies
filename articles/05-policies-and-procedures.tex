\chapter{Policies and Procedures}

\section{Policies}

The purpose of policies served as the official guidelines and framework
for the Board of Executives to make informed decisions and take actions
that are consistent with the Association's mission and values.

\subsection{Board Authority to Establish Policies}
The Board of Executives is authorized to establish, amend, and 
enforce policies and procedures consistent with this constitution 
to govern the operational affairs of the Association.

\subsection{Matters Subject to Policy}
The Board may establish policies regarding:
\begin{enumerate}

    \item Operational procedures and implementation guidelines,
    
    \item Meeting conduct and participation requirements,
    
    \item Financial management and expenditure guidelines,
    
    \item Event planning and resource allocation,
    
    \item Committee formation and standing committee operations, and
    
    \item Other matters necessary for the effective operation of the Association.

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Types of Policies}
Policies may be established in the following forms:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Policy Statements: Policies that provide high-level principles and 
    guidelines for the Board's decision-making and actions on specific matters.

    \item Procedure Policies: Policies that provide detailed steps and 
    guidelines for the implementation of specific policies established by the Board.

    \item Committee Policies: Policies that govern the formation, operation, and
    responsibilities of standing committees of the Association.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Constitutional Supremacy}
All policies and procedures shall be subordinate to this Constitution. 
In the event of conflict between a policy and this Constitution, 
the Constitution shall prevail.

\subsection{Mission Statement Guidance}
Policies shall be consistent with the Association's current Mission Statement
and shall not contradict the values and principles outlined therein.

\subsection{Policy Requirements}
Policies shall be clear, specific, and actionable through Procedures
Policies shall provide rational and sufficient guidance for implementation 
and enforcement by the Board of Executives.

\subsection{Policy Adoption Process}
Policies may be adopted by the Board of Executives through majority vote, 
provided that notice and a reasonable opportunity for discussion have been 
given to Board members.

\subsection{Member Notice}
Adopted policies shall be made available to general members and maintained 
as a record accessible to all members.

\subsection{Policy Amendment}
Policies may be amended or repealed by the Board of Executives in the 
same manner as adoption.

\subsection{Policy Review and Enforcement}
The Board shall periodically review existing policies to ensure their continued
relevance and consistency with the Constitution and the Association's mission.

\section{Standard Operating Procedures}

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) are detailed, step-by-step instructions
that outline the specific actions and processes required to implement policies
established by the Board of Executives. SOPs provide clear guidance to ensure
consistent and effective execution of the policies in practice and other 
operational matters of the Association.

\subsection{Board Authority to Establish Procedures}
The Board of Executives is authorized to establish, amend, and 
enforce SOPs consistent with this Constitution and the policies
established by the Board to govern the operational affairs of the Association.

\subsection{SOPs Consistent with Policies}
SOPs shall be consistent with the policies established by the Board
and shall provide detailed guidance on the implementation of those policies.

\subsection{SOP Adoption and Amendment}
SOPs may be adopted and amended by the Board of Executives either through
discretionary authority or through a majority vote, provided that notice and a
reasonable opportunity for discussion have been given to Board members.
