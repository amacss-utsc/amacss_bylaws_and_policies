\chapter{Amendments}

Amendments to this Constitution may be proposed and adopted in accordance
 with the procedures outlined in this Article. The purpose of amendments 
 is to allow for necessary changes and improvements to the governance 
 structure and operations of the Association while ensuring that such 
 changes are made through a transparent and democratic process.

 The power of amendment is vested in the general membership of the Association, 
 and any amendment to this Constitution shall be subject to ratification by a 
 majority of the general membership.

\section{Amendment Procedures}

\subsection{Amendments Proposal}
Amendments to this Constitution may be proposed by any general member 
of the Association through a petition. Amendments may also be proposed by
any member of the Board of Executives without requirement of a petition.

\subsection{Board Review}
The Board shall review and consider all proposed amendments and may either approve or reject them. 
The process of reviewing a petition or proposed amendment by the Board are outlined in \S\ref{petition-process} 
of this Constitution.

\subsection{Call for Special Meeting of Members}
If a proposed amendment is approved by the Board, the Board shall call for 
a special meeting of the general membership to discuss and vote on the proposed amendment. 
The special meeting shall be held within a reasonable time frame after the approval of the 
proposed amendment by the Board, and all general members shall be given reasonable notice 
and opportunity to participate in the meeting.

\section{Ratification}

\subsection{Vote by Referendum}
An amendment to this Constitution shall be voted on through a referendum 
and ratifies if it receives a simple majority of votes cast held among 
the general membership of the Association. The referendum for ratification 
of an amendment shall follow the same procedures outlined in 
\S\ref{referendum-process} of this Constitution.

\subsection{Ratification}
If an amendment is ratified by the general membership, it shall become
effective immediately upon the announcement of the referendum results, unless
otherwise specified in the amendment itself. The amendment shall then be incorporated
into this Constitution and shall be binding on all members of the Association.
